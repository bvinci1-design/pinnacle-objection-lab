Pinnacle Objection Lab

For Certified Pinnacle Business Guides · Objection practice

Owners raise the same handful of concerns in almost every first conversation, from cost and timing to “we already run EOS®.” This is a practice space for those moments: what you'll hear, what it usually means, and a respectful path from the objection to a next step.

Never knock the alternativeEOS®, their coach, or doing it themselves. Describe the difference and let them name the flaws.
Ask before you tellOne open question before any point you make.
Land one pointOne clear difference beats five.
Respect a noLeave every conversation with the door open.

    Where the time goes

    1. Connect Why they're talking to you, and a frame for the conversation.
    2. Understand Where they are, what it's costing them, what they've tried, and where they want to be. Most of the conversation.
    3. Bridge Play back what they want, in their words, and ask permission to go on.
    4. Show Only the parts of Pinnacle that close the gap they described.
    5. Agree A next step they choose, with room to say no.
